Friday

A personal AI assistant, running privately on its owner's own hardware.

What Friday is

Friday is a personal AI assistant that its owner talks to through a command line and a private messaging interface. It handles day-to-day tasks on the owner's behalf — reading and triaging email, managing a calendar, working with documents and spreadsheets, and keeping track of files — so those tasks can be handled conversationally instead of by clicking through web interfaces.

Friday runs entirely on hardware owned and operated by its owner. It is not a hosted product and it is not offered to the public.

Who uses Friday

Exactly one person: the owner of the Google account that grants it access. There is no public sign-up, no multi-tenant service, and no way for any other person to authenticate to it. Friday exists purely to automate its owner's own account.

What Friday does with a Google account

  • Gmail — read and search mail, and create draft replies for the owner to review and send manually.
  • Google Calendar — read and create the owner's own calendar events.
  • Google Drive — find and upload files to the owner's own Drive.
  • Sheets and Docs — read and write spreadsheets and documents the owner asks it to work on.
  • Contacts — look up the owner's contacts when addressing mail.

Every action is initiated by the owner, in the owner's own session, against the owner's own data. Friday never sends mail, shares files, or modifies anything external without an explicit instruction from the owner.

How data is handled

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own self-hosted machine. OAuth credentials are kept in a local file readable only by the owner's operating-system account. Nothing is transmitted to any third party, there is no analytics, no telemetry, and no external server operated by this application. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
